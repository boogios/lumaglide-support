LumaGlide Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026 · BOOGIOS_STUDIO

No personal data collected by the game

LumaGlide is an offline, paid puzzle game. It has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking, social feed, or in-app purchases. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data through the game.

Information stored on your device

Puzzle progress, an unfinished puzzle, completed daily gardens, and your language, theme, sound and haptic preferences are stored locally on your device. The game does not transmit these records to our servers. Removing the app removes its local data. Operating-system backups are managed by Apple under your device settings.

Apple and external links

Apple manages the App Store purchase, payment processing, optional diagnostic reporting, and system backups according to Apple's policies. We do not receive your payment card details. Opening support, privacy or developer links leaves the game and uses your browser; those services have their own privacy policies.

Support

If you contact us voluntarily, we use the message and information you provide to answer your request. Please do not send payment card details or sensitive information. Contact: @by.sangwoo on Instagram.

Children

The game does not ask children or other players to provide personal information, and contains no chat or user-generated content.

Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this policy and its effective date.

LumaGlide는 오프라인 유료 퍼즐 게임입니다. 계정, 광고, 분석 SDK, 추적, 앱 내 구매가 없으며 게임을 통해 개인정보를 수집하거나 전송하지 않습니다. 퍼즐 진행 상황과 설정은 기기에만 저장됩니다. 앱을 삭제하면 로컬 데이터도 삭제됩니다. Apple의 결제·백업·진단 기능과 외부 링크는 해당 서비스의 정책을 따릅니다. 지원을 위해 직접 보낸 메시지는 문의 응답에만 사용합니다.

プライバシーポリシー

LumaGlideはオフラインの有料パズルゲームです。アカウント、広告、分析SDK、追跡、アプリ内課金はありません。ゲームから個人情報を収集・送信しません。進捗と設定は端末内に保存されます。Appleの決済・バックアップ・診断および外部リンクには各サービスのポリシーが適用されます。お問い合わせで提供された情報は回答のために使用します。
