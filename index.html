LumaGlide

A little light. A quiet journey.

Guide a glowing seed through 144 garden puzzles. Gather all three fireflies, then stop on the garden ring. Stones stop your glide; moss pads stop you early; landing on a portal sends you to its partner.

Support

Contact @by.sangwoo on Instagram for help. Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version and garden number. Never send payment details.

Common questions

Stuck? Undo, restart, and hints are always available. A route may become unsolvable until you undo or restart.

Missing progress? Progress is stored on the device. Reinstalling the game or switching devices may remove local progress. There is no game account or cloud sync.

Sounds? Use Garden sounds in Settings. The game respects your iPhone's silent mode.

Stars? The shortest route without hints earns three stars. A longer route without hints earns two. Using hints earns one. Undo does not reduce stars.

Daily garden? The puzzle follows your device's local calendar date. There are 366 distinct daily boards, reused in subsequent years. No online ranking or time limit.

Purchases? One App Store purchase includes all campaign boards and hints. No ads or in-app purchases. Apple handles refunds and purchase history.
